"THE WORLD BY REGION" — a cream broadsheet front page summing up Friday 20 November 2026 in 40 stories, organised by region. A giant serif date masthead, a navy region bar (Asia-Pacific 18 · Europe 7 · Americas 9 · Mideast 2 · Africa 2 · Oceania 2), a day-in-numbers strip (300,000 evacuated · $1.8bn GTA VI · −1,100 Dow · 16bn miles Voyager · 9–6 ICJ vote), three lead stories with coloured header bands (dark navy / teal / purple) and flat illustrations (Akvan comes ashore, Trump puts the Iran decision off to 2027, a missile over Riyadh), ten secondary stories in a grid, a "WHERE IT HAPPENED" world map with numbered pins, the ALSO ON list (14–40) in three columns, and a DATE CHECK box listing the 19 November stories deliberately left out. Every headline and number is exact programmatic text; all illustrations are flat vector-style drawings made with Python PIL — no photos, no copied art.
